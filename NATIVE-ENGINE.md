# NIKO Native V5 candidate

The live app now imports niko-native.js and the angle-based NikoGesture. The old engine.js remains in Git and its regression tests, but is excluded from the static build and service worker. NOTICE.txt and LICENSE.md are unchanged: this is not a legal determination of independent authorship, and no existing MIT rights are withdrawn. The landmark conventions and other third-party dependencies remain applicable. The old source was inspected for compatibility; no certified clean-room process.

Cadence uses bilateral knee-flexion angle differences, timestamp-based smoothing, alternating threshold crossings, median inter-event interval and dispersion rejection. Counts are detected alternations, not measured footsteps. The estimator assumes alternating knee motion and can misinterpret other rhythmic movements; no physical reference or clinical validation. Low-rate, occluded or irregular signals can be rejected. Thresholds are experimental. The existing measurement gate and encrypted export remain. Robot commands retain their existing virtual UI semantics; no physical actuation or claim of a fully independent robot engine.

Synthetic comparison is limited to one knee-phase fixture; it does not establish superior real-world accuracy. Prior source code and tests remain accessible in the repository. No change of license or deployment without approval.

## V5.1 — Quality gate
Interpolated crossing timestamps; same-phase half-period estimation; rejects irregular cycles and sampling gaps above 160 ms. Synthetic benchmark: 4 known rates (90/120/150/180), 3 noise amplitudes, deterministic seeds, irregular sampling; acceptance coverage >=75%, MAE <=5 alternations/min; zero outputs on simultaneous flexion and stationary controls. Legacy results are logged for comparison without claiming clinical superiority. This is a regression fixture, not a held-out human dataset. Actual video and reference instrument validation remain mandatory. Low frame-rate devices may receive fewer estimates deliberately.

## V6 adaptive candidate
Robust rolling quantiles estimate the center and threshold of bilateral knee phase differences. The estimator requires sufficient amplitude on each side and negative correlation, rejecting unilateral and simultaneous motion. It keeps bounded 3.2 s history and recomputes statistics every 250 ms. Old V5.1 remains a comparison baseline. Candidate tests add low-amplitude and asymmetric offset synthetic fixtures, plus standard and negative controls. These fixtures are not a clinical dataset, and bilateral image landmarks are not independent sensors. Application still requires bilateral visibility and a six-second quality window. No claim of superiority until comparative CI results are available; no measured CPU or physical camera performance yet.

## IBM Quantum experiment (not submitted)
`npm run --silent quantum:tune` evaluates four actual V6 parameter configurations on synthetic training and separate synthetic holdout cases. The default production candidate is unchanged. `node scripts/quantum-tune.mjs --circuit > circuit.json` exports a two-qubit, one-layer, fixed-angle QAOA circuit for the existing AutoCoder `quantum_hardware.py` bridge. With an authenticated IBM Open Plan account, run bridge `prepare circuit.json --shots 256`, then `submit circuit.json --shots 256 --approve <digest>` and retrieve `result <jobId>`. Save the result to JSON and decode with `node scripts/quantum-tune.mjs --decode result.json`. No hardware submission from CI. The CPU exhaustively solves all four choices, so no quantum advantage can be claimed. Counts alone are not hardware evidence; retain IBM job ID, backend and completed status. The objective weights are engineering choices, not clinical thresholds.

## V6.1 — Classical tuning candidate
IBM experiment abandoned by owner; no QPU job was submitted. Historical experiment remains reproducible with default tau=65 ms/fraction=.22. CPU exhaustive four-option search selected tau=65 ms/fraction=.18 on its training objective; synthetic holdout MAE was .3480699646 vs .3833318986 for baseline, both full coverage and zero false outputs on tested negatives. Selection is explicitly passed by the application, not an automatic model update. Added distinct synthetic test profiles with harmonic distortion, offsets, aspect ratios and seeded noise; acceptance thresholds predeclared in tests, not clinical. Export records the chosen parameters and names the count detected_alternations, not steps (breaking JSON key change, versioned candidate). No medical validation, hardware benchmark, real camera or quantum execution claimed.

V6.1 UI and session metadata explicitly use detected knee alternations/minute, not footsteps. Existing per-frame cadence_spm field is retained for compatibility; the top-level cadence_unit defines its meaning.

## V6.2 — Phase-specific regression candidate
A common timestamp slope with separate phase intercepts is fitted after the existing robust median consistency gate. Minimum eight valid alternating crossings, bounded history, fallback to median if disagreement exceeds 8%; no previously rejected frames are rescued. Existing median modes remain reproducible. New benchmark compares against V6.1 on 16 positive synthetic conditions and 3 negative controls, requiring lower aggregate MAE and preserved coverage. No clinical or physical-camera accuracy claims; not released until comparative CI succeeds. Developed classically, no IBM or Agnes execution.

## V6.5 — Robust Vision research
Adds an optional projected-segment consistency guard using a bounded median history. Gross geometry outliers are rejected with no estimate and no invented landmarks. Short isolated rejections preserve prior valid history, but existing 160 ms continuity and visibility gates remain; low visibility resets immediately. Aspect changes reset history. Twelve synthetic gross-outlier conditions and clean controls measure coverage, error, abstention cost and dropout recovery. This tests injected landmark errors, not actual lighting, camera perspective or human occlusion performance; real camera tests remain necessary. Application stays on V6.4 pending validation. The thresholds are experimental.
